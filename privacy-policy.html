Mahjong Jing 麻雀精 Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Mahjong Jing 麻雀精 ("the app") is published by Mahjong Jing 麻雀精. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, and why.

No account, no personal data collected by us

The app requires no sign-up, login, or account of any kind. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information. Your puzzle progress, streaks, and collected pins are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by a third-party advertising network. To deliver and measure ads, that network's software development kit (SDK) may collect device identifiers and general usage data (such as which ads were shown and for how long). This data is used to serve and measure ads, and is handled by the ad network under its own privacy policy.

The app uses rewarded video ads in two places, and both are entirely optional — you are never required to watch one to keep playing. Watching a rewarded ad can unlock an extra attempt on that day's puzzle, or earn a Pass you can use to make up a day you missed. The app does not use rewarded ads for anything beyond these two, and does not sell in-app purchases tied to advertising.

Data shared with third parties

We do not sell your data. The only third party involved is the advertising network described above, which may receive device and usage identifiers solely to serve and measure ads.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to care@sensiblywild.com.
