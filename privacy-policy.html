Mahjong Jing 麻雀精

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Published by Sensibly Wild, Hong Kong

The short version

  • No sign-up. We never ask for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your progress, streaks and pins stay on your phone.
  • Ads are shown by a third-party ad network, which may collect device identifiers to serve and measure them.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Mahjong Jing 麻雀精 ("the app") is a Hong Kong mahjong puzzle game made by Sensibly Wild ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we don't collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-up. We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, daily streak, tier, collected pins, personal records and settings are saved only on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Today's date from our server

So the daily puzzle changes at midnight Hong Kong time for everyone, the app asks our small date service, hosted on Cloudflare, for today's date and game settings. The request contains no personal information or progress. Like any website, Cloudflare receives your device's IP address to deliver the response; we don't store it or use it to identify you.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your phone's Settings.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads. Ads are provided by a third-party ad network. Its software may collect device identifiers (such as your device's advertising identifier) and general usage information, such as which ads were shown and for how long. The ad network uses this to show and measure ads under its own privacy policy.

On iPhone, if the app asks to track you and you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads will not use your advertising identifier to personalise ads. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Rewarded ads

Watching a rewarded video is always optional. It can unlock an extra attempt on that day's puzzle, or earn a Pass to make up a missed day. Rewarded ads aren't used for anything else, and the app doesn't sell ad-related in-app purchases.

Sharing your information

We don't sell your data. The only third parties involved are the ad network, which receives device and usage information to serve and measure ads, and Cloudflare, which delivers the date service.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version at this address with a new effective date.
